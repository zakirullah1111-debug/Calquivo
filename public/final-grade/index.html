How it works

Required final = (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight, with weight expressed as a decimal.

Worked example

An 80% current grade, 30% final, and 85% target require 96.67% on the final. Displayed required scores are rounded up to two decimals.

What if the required score is above 100%?

The target cannot be reached under this model without extra credit or a changed grading policy.

Does this account for a minimum passing final score?

No. Check your syllabus for separate requirements; this tool only models the overall weighted average.
