Final replaces lowest exam

Calculate a target when your final also replaces one lower midterm score.

Midterms are equally weighted. The final replaces exactly one lowest midterm only if higher, and also counts separately at its final-exam weight. All midterms must be eligible for replacement.

Two effects from one exam

The calculator replaces the lowest midterm with the final score when that improves it, recalculates the midterm average, then adds the final and other-work contributions.

Worked example

For midterms of 60, 75, and 80, a 50% midterm weight, a 30% final weight, and 90% on the remaining 20%, a final of 77.50% reaches an overall 80%.

Check your policy

This model does not cover replacing an exam with the average of two scores, unequal midterm weights, or replacing an ineligible missed exam. It is a scenario tool, not a university-specific calculator.
