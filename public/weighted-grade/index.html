Weighted grade calculator

Combine category averages, optionally dropping the lowest percentage scores.

Each row is one category. Enter comma-separated percentages and its share of the course. Scores within a category have equal weight. This is not the Canvas points-impact drop rule.

Weights must total 100%. Missing future work is not inferred: enter complete category projections for a projected course grade.

Calculation method

For each category, sort percentage scores from lowest to highest, remove the requested count, and average the remaining scores. Multiply each category average by its weight and add the contributions.

Worked example

Quizzes of 60, 80, and 100 with the lowest dropped average 90%. At a 40% weight, combined with an exam grade of 80% weighted 60%, the result is 84%.

Unequal point values?

This tool gives each score equal weight within its category. If your course adds raw points or drops whichever assignment improves the total most, use your instructor’s method instead.
